### Events

There are several regular events at Penn State that are of interest to researchers in Computational Biology and Bioinformatics. Some of these include:

* [Bioinformatics and Genomics Retreats](https://www.huck.psu.edu/graduate-programs/bioinformatics-and-genomics/activities/annual-retreat)
* [Center for Medical Genomics retreats](https://www.google.com/search?q=psu+center+for+medical+genomics+retreat)
* [Weekly Wednesday Wartik Genomics Lecture Series (WWWGLS)](../seminar/)


### Related Penn State organizations


* [Center for Medical Genomics](http://www.bx.psu.edu/ctr_med_genom/)
* [Center for Eukaryotic Gene Regulation](http://www.cegr.psu.edu/)
* [Genome Sciences Institute of the Huck](https://www.huck.psu.edu/content/research/genome-sciences-institute)
* [Graduate Student Organization for Genomics interest (GenoMIX)](https://www.huck.psu.edu/graduate-programs/bioinformatics-and-genomics/activities/genomix-graduate-student-organization-for-genomics): a forum for graduate students whose research is related to bioinformatics and genomics
* [Bioinformatics Consulting Center](https://www.ialbert.me/consulting.html): offers bioinformatics data analysis services that cover multiple application domains of high throughput sequencing. 
* [PSU's Genomics Core Sequencing Facilities](https://www.huck.psu.edu/content/instrumentation-facilities/genomics-core-facility): provides next-generation sequencing and a number of traditional genomic services to the Penn State community as well as those outside the University
