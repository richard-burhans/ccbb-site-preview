# 2018 Workshop on emerging methods for sequence analysis

CCBB will host a one-day workshop on emerging methods for sequence analysis. The workshop will feature a mix of invited speakers and local Penn State speakers. 
It will be held on June 22nd (2018), immediately following the [PSU Boot Camp on Data Reproducibility](TODO).
The workshop is organized by [Qunhua Li](http://www.personal.psu.edu/users/q/u/qul12/), [Shaun Mahony](http://mahonylab.org/), [Paul Medvedev](http://medvedevgroup.com/medvedev.html), and [Anton Nekrutenko](https://nekrut.github.io/lab_site/).


Registration is free. Please register [here](https://goo.gl/forms/KzkaUhVxp9MmqxsV2) by May 25th 5PM. Breakfast and lunch will be provided for registered participants. 
The location of the workshop is the [ASI](http://www.psumap.com/#Agricultural%20Science%20%26%20Industries) auditorium. 

The invited talks are:

|:-----|:-----|
| Elena Fertig (invited) | Enter the matrix: factorization uncovers knowledge from omics |
| Seyoung Kim (invited) | Statistical methods for learning gene networks under SNP perturbation |
| Adam Phillippy (invited) | Can nanopore sequencing finally finish the human genome? |
