# 2023 Workshop on emerging methods for sequence analysis

CCBB will host a one-day workshop on emerging methods for sequence analysis. The workshop will feature a mix of invited speakers and talks selected from submitted abstracts.  
It will be held on May 24, 2023. 

## Organizers
* [Stefan Canzar](https://www.genzentrum.uni-muenchen.de/research-groups/canzar/index.html)
* [Paul Medvedev](http://medvedevgroup.com/medvedev.html)
* [Mingfu Shao](https://sites.psu.edu/mxs2589/)
